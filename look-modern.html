III. Getting started

How do I start a project with Sleepouts Galore?

Getting started takes three simple steps, and it all begins with a conversation.

  1. Get in touch

    Call 021 106 2900 or email bill@sleepoutsgalore.co.nz. You can also message us on Facebook.

  2. Share the details

    Tell us where in Christchurch the work is and what you're hoping to build. Photos, sketches or measurements of the space all help, along with any timing you have in mind.

  3. Talk it through

    We'll talk through your idea and work out the next steps together, whether that's a sleepout or another building or carpentry job.
